About

Built for designers.
Open to the creative.

Why Crelume exists.

Crelume started because design in Nigeria has talent but no home. Too many designers learning alone, creating alone, getting stuck alone. We wanted a community where designers can learn, build, and belong — from Aba to the world.

Vision

To become the home for designers across Africa — where creativity is nurtured, talent is seen, and work moves culture forward.

Mission

To equip designers with resources, community, and real opportunities to grow, collaborate, and create work that matters.

Values

Our Values

People First

Designers are not tools. We grow people before we grow products.

Build in Public

We learn openly, share openly, and grow openly.

Excellence Over Voice

We care about craft, not clout.

Who it's For

Aspiring Designers
